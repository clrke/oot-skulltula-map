    About

    The locations, numbering and item requirements come from Nintendo Life's guide “Zelda: Ocarina of Time: All Gold Skulltula Locations & Rewards” by Olliemar28. Please visit it for full walkthroughs and screenshots.

    Visits are counted with Google Analytics; your checklist stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

    The game itself does not number Gold Skulltulas; the numbers here are the guide's own ordering. The hints are short paraphrases written for this tool, and ages marked “inferred” were worked out from the guide's directions and items rather than stated by it.
